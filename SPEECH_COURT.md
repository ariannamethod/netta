# NETTA SPEECH COURT — preregistration (frozen before any judged byte)

domain: arianna-method.netta.body1-speech-court/v1

This court judges Body 1 speech. It is preregistered while no Body 1
binary exists. Construction and iteration may look at speech freely;
no spoken byte is JUDGED, and the word "coherence" is not used for
Body 1, until this court sits with two hands.

## Ruler (sealed now)

1. **Ear.** An independent ear prices each spoken stream in bits/byte
   against honest ignorance over the same inventory, with the ear's own
   tables built from the same world by its own hand. Cheaper than
   ignorance is necessary, never sufficient.
2. **Anti-copy census.** Longest-match of the spoken stream against the
   lived tape, and quoted-coverage against the committed void line of
   Body 0's verdict: coverage at or above 0.50 of any speech stream by
   verbatim tape runs voids that stream (`body0/verdict.md`, the frozen
   0.50 line). Copying is not speech.
3. **The triple.** Every measurement runs three mouths from identical
   seeds: advised (live Court-4 citizens), plain (no citizens), and
   shuffled-citizens null. The citizens' effect is the difference, and a
   null effect is printed as a result, not hidden.
4. **Speech is shown.** The court's report carries every judged stream
   verbatim. A numbers-only speech verdict is unlawful (MOUTH_PROTOCOL
   §6).

## Verdict form

PASS requires: every judged stream beats honest ignorance under the
ear; no stream voided by the anti-copy line; restart identity holds
(same flags and seed, byte-identical speech); both hands agree on the
census. The literal verdict line is one of:

    SPEECH PASS: the mouth speaks below ignorance and above copying
    SPEECH FAIL: <named reason>

Thresholds enter only from committed records, each named in the
verdict. Iteration between sittings is lawful and unlimited; each
sitting is one measurement of the then-current mouth.
