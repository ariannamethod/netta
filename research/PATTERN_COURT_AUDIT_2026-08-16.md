# Pattern-court audit and the public warrant

Date: 2026-08-16.  The untouched `f38c5cf` body-24 binary and all 220
inherited gates were reproduced before its diff was read.  The audit findings
below were measured on that binary.  The body-25 law and predictions were
written after those failures were found and before any repair was implemented
or rerun.

## Independent audit findings

The four-word lattice and its causal restraint are sound on Don's calibration
worlds.  A 1600-byte quilt made from exact fifteen-byte shore fragments, each
separated by one foreign byte, has matched-16 coverage zero and structure gap
1.356447; `order` is the honest verdict.  An equal-census stream made from 100
`a`, 100 `b`, then 100 `c` bytes reads `stranger`: this is the declared limit
of a trigram court, not a hidden census verdict.  Context is real jurisdiction:
candidate `ab` is cold `stranger` at G=0.272754 but becomes `order` under
explicit context `cb` at G=1.670511.  Both lines name `cold` or `6362`, so the
conditional verdict is public.

Two failures are constitutional rather than statistical.

First, the public threshold contradicts itself.  On the period-3 shore, 1000
literal bytes followed by 1000 foreign bytes report `matched16=50.0%` and
`replay`.  Appending one more foreign byte gives exact coverage 1000/2001 =
49.975012... percent, but the one-decimal output still reports
`matched16=50.0%` while the verdict changes to `order`.  Two visible identical
operands produce two verdicts.  The internal `>=` comparison is correct; the
published witness is insufficient to reconstruct it.

Second, raw byte movement is not measured structural jurisdiction.  A
4096-byte shore with one `b` among 4095 `a` bytes grows a non-identical twin:
the lone `b` moves, so `twin-changed=2/4096`.  Against 300 `a` bytes, however,
true and twin prices are bit-identical at 0.087545 and G=0.000000.  The court
does not print `changed`, still passes its hidden `changed != 0` door, and says
`replay` solely from the perfect match.  The constant-shore constitution says
a null that cannot move the measurement must not claim structural
jurisdiction; moving irrelevant bytes is not enough.

## Body 25 preregistration: the public warrant

Body 25 is the first, deliberately narrow clause of the future earning law.
It grants no action power.  It says only that a court must earn the right to
render a non-abstaining public verdict by publishing every operand that
determines it and by showing that its falsifier moved the measurement in the
case actually heard.

The pattern court keeps its four frozen words and thresholds.  Its public
resolution is the six decimal places already promised for G: one microbit per
byte.  The implementation will therefore publish and decide from an integer
`gap-micro`, rounded once from the measured total gap.  It will also publish
the twin price Q, exact `matched-bytes/bytes`, and exact
`twin-changed/shore-bytes` beside the human-readable percentages.

The lattice is evaluated from those public integers in this order:

1. `abstain` if the twin changed zero bytes **or** `gap-micro == 0`.  The first
   door says the null did not move the shore; the second says it did not move
   this candidate's measured coordinate at the court's declared resolution.
2. `replay` if `2 * matched-bytes >= candidate-bytes`.  This is the frozen
   inclusive fifty-percent law without a floating comparison.
3. `order` if `gap-micro >= 500000`, the frozen inclusive 0.5-bit law on the
   published ruler.
4. `stranger` otherwise.

This is not a near-degeneracy fraction chosen after seeing 2/4096.  No changed
fraction threshold is introduced.  The warrant asks the falsifier a more
direct exact question: did it change the coordinate on which this verdict
depends at all at the already-declared public resolution?

Before the repaired binary is run, the predictions are:

- exact 1000/2000 remains `replay`; 1000/2001 remains `order`, but their
  printed percentages and exact fractions are visibly different;
- the one-`b` shore over 300 `a` bytes prints `changed=2/4096`,
  `gap-micro=0`, and changes from `replay` to `abstain`;
- body 24's literal replay, independently written replay, order, stranger,
  constant abstention, straddle, and context verdicts otherwise remain;
- the fifteen-byte quilt remains `order`, and the equal-census stream remains
  the named `stranger` that asks for a separate future census body;
- all invocation, no-write, state-v20, convoy, and sanitizer laws remain.

The public warrant is necessary but not sufficient for authority.  A later
earning law still needs external preregistration receipts, independent review,
red-world scope, a named requested power, revocation, and a separately enacted
activation body.  Netta cannot legitimately self-attest those historical and
social facts merely by printing a confident line from inside herself.

## Results

The repaired body was first built under the strict C11 warning law.  The two
threshold worlds now publish distinct evidence and retain their predicted
verdicts:

```
bytes=2000 P=4.225847 Q=5.070682 matched16=50.0000% matched-bytes=1000/2000 G=0.844835 gap-micro=844835 changed=2822/4200 verdict=replay
bytes=2001 P=4.227733 Q=5.072146 matched16=49.9750% matched-bytes=1000/2001 G=0.844413 gap-micro=844413 changed=2822/4200 verdict=order
```

The one-`b` null also landed exactly on the prediction: `P=Q=0.087545`,
`matched-bytes=300/300`, `gap-micro=0`, `changed=2/4096`, and `abstain`.
The fifteen-byte quilt remains `order` at gap-micro 1356447; the equal-census
stream remains `stranger` at gap-micro -5757493; and the named `cb` context
still changes candidate `ab` from cold `stranger` to contextual `order`.

One preregistered prediction failed, and the failure strengthens rather than
weakens the warrant.  Body 24's 300-byte all-`z` foreign stream was predicted
to remain `stranger`.  Instead its twin changes 2822/4200 shore bytes while
both candidate prices are exactly 8.013738 at public resolution, so
gap-micro is zero.  Under the law written above the only admissible result is
`abstain`.  The inherited stranger expectation was replaced by an executable
gate for that abstention; the law was not weakened after seeing the result.
The separate equal-census stranger world demonstrates that `stranger` remains
reachable when the null actually moves the measured coordinate.

All 225 gates pass, including the five new red worlds and ASan/UBSan through
the court.  State remains v20, and the court still writes nothing and grants
nothing.
