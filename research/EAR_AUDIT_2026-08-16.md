# Island-ear audit

Date: 2026-08-16.  This section was written on `sol/zero-turn-8` before
the cross-shore hearings and before implementing body 21.  It fixes the
questions and failure meanings so that another pleasant Dracula number cannot
choose the law after the fact.

## Preregistered audit

The body-20 implementation is accepted only if the ear is a function of the
named immutable shore and candidate stream.  Explicit life controls must be
refused rather than ignored, ambient default state/biography files must not
affect a hearing, a candidate of exactly 16384 bytes must be heard, and a
16385-byte candidate must be refused without truncation.  Exact quotation is
checked at both stream boundaries and across the sixteen-byte threshold.

The sorted-twin gate is world-local, not a theorem: on the existing period-3
world sorting should be more expensive, while on a block-sorted world the
sorted candidate should be cheaper than an alternating census twin.  Either
outcome is evidence about a named shore only.

Cold hearing prices byte zero by the unigram book and byte one by the bigram
book before entering the trigram book.  A constructed `a...azzbbbb` shore is
expected to rank `aa` over `bb` when cold but reverse that ranking after an
explicit `zz` context.  If so, the warmup is not an error, but an unnamed
experimental variable.  Body 21 may carry a two-byte context into the ear
only if the cold instrument remains available and both modes stay powerless.

Finally, independently grown 384000-byte Frankenstein and technical lives
will each speak 300 bytes from locked uni, bi, and tri hands under both the
supported and Laplace laws, prompt `The ` and speech seed 7.  Before hearing,
the prediction is: on each hand's own shore the supported tri stream prices
below supported bi, supported bi below supported uni, every supported stream
prices below its matched Laplace twin, and no stream has a sixteen-byte exact
quotation.  Any failed inequality narrows the Dracula conclusion; none can be
discarded and no result grants speech authority.

## Audit verdict

The untouched `6d9ee94` binary reproduced all 175 inherited gates.  Three
implementation defects then reproduced outside the suite: `--seed 99` was
silently accepted by an ear invocation; a candidate of exactly 16384 bytes
was refused because a full `fread` had not yet set EOF; and two hardlinked
ambient files named `netta0.state` and `netta0.bio.tsv` aborted a hearing
before `ear()` despite neither being named on the command line.

The repair gives the ear one complete invocation law: every explicit life or
mouth control is refused, even at its default value.  Hearing now occurs
before all life-only alias and state checks.  A one-byte-overread separates
the inclusive 16384-byte boundary from a longer candidate without truncation.
The descending substring DP survived matches at both tape boundaries, the
15/16 threshold, and embedded NUL.  It is an exact longest-common-substring
instrument at the tested boundary.

The period-3 sorted red is not universal, as preregistered.  On a block-sorted
`a...ab...b` shore, a sorted equal-census stream prices at 3.230624 bits/byte
against 7.208411 for its alternating twin.  The log now names both outcomes
as local facts rather than promoting either into a theorem.

The warmup counterworld also landed as predicted.  Cold, `aa` beats `bb`
(1.836756 against 6.097363 bits/byte); after explicit context `zz`, `bb`
beats `aa` (7.005625 against 8.002812).  Body 21 therefore adds
`--ear-context P`: the last two bytes of a named immutable file enter the
trigram context and are printed in the verdict.  Fewer than two bytes are
refused.  With no context flag, body 20's uni/bi cold warmup is preserved
exactly.  Both modes remain read-only measurements with no threshold or
authority, and state remains v20.

## Two further shores

The sealed normalized Frankenstein and technical tapes were verified against
their published byte lengths and SHA-256 witnesses.  Each new life was grown
independently for 48 episodes of 8000 bytes at seed 160816.  Locked uni, bi,
and tri hands then spoke 300 bytes under supported and Laplace laws with
prompt `The ` and speech seed 7; the context-bearing local ear heard all
twelve streams.  The complete table is
`ear_results/2026-08-16-cross-shore.tsv`, including a SHA-256 witness for
every 300-byte stream.

The price prediction passed on both shores.  Frankenstein ranks supported
tri 2.910738, bi 4.992303, uni 6.995211 bits/byte; technical ranks tri
3.134352, bi 5.268368, uni 7.563339.  Every supported hand beats its matched
Laplace twin.  Thus the Dracula ordering reproduced twice, but it remains a
three-shore result, not a speech court.

The no-sixteen-byte-match prediction failed once.  Technical supported tri
has longest match 17 and matched-16 coverage 5.7 percent.  The exact bytes
are seventeen spaces at speech offset 223 and shore offset 8956.  This is a
more important semantic finding than a threshold failure: exact substring
establishes shared bytes, not the causal fact of quotation.  The code and
current documentation therefore say `longest-match` and `matched16`; the
pre-audit names survive above as the falsified claim.  A future copying court
will need a preregistered complexity control.  Body 21 does not invent one
after seeing this result.

The original descending DP was exact but cost the product of shore and
candidate lengths: about fourteen billion cells at the public cap on
Dracula.  It is replaced by a suffix automaton of the bounded candidate,
scanned once by each shore.  Its per-candidate-end answers were checked
against brute force on 190400 deterministic random small worlds; the
permanent gates retain tape boundaries, NUL, the 15/16 threshold, and an
overlapping coverage union.  A 16384-byte hearing on the full 855114-byte
Dracula shore then completed in 0.38 seconds wall time on the audit host.

With these repairs and the context-bearing ear, 190 gates pass including
ASan/UBSan.  No checkpoint field changed.
