## 5. Both hands and what PASS means

The existing independent reader rebuilds Alice's unit inventory, replays the
support and corridor laws, reconstructs every spoken byte from each trace,
and applies the unchanged ear and 0.50 anti-copy ruler. Restart identity
covers speech and trace for all fifteen streams.

The crossing reader then authenticates all inputs and reports. For every
seed, live speech must differ bytewise from both its plain and shuffled mate,
and the independently reconstructed path must locate at least one choice at
which the live citizen changed the sampling distribution inside its exact
earned context. No influence outside that context is admissible.

The literal outcomes are:

    ALICE PASS: earned citizens are audible in lawful foreign speech
    ALICE NULL: earned citizens are not audibly distinct on the frozen seeds
    ALICE FAIL: <named identity, support, restart, ear, or anti-copy breach>

PASS means only audible, bounded influence without voiding the foreign mouth.
It does not rank live speech above either control, prove semantic wholeness,
earn the word coherence, or enlarge Court 4's one-relation boundary.
