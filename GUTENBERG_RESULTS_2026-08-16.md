The kin prediction passed strongly and at every individual window: transfer
grew from byte frequency to one-byte context to two-byte context. But the

structure; genre distance is not an alien world.

The shuffled prediction also failed. Its contextual excess was `0.245653`
for byte-bi and `0.360804` for byte-tri, above the sealed `0.1` ceiling.
This does not mean the shuffled target retained its prose order: every byte
histogram is exact and its witness prices are very poor in absolute terms.
It means that subtracting the atomic gain did not isolate all reusable
conditional prior carried by the trained context rows. The old synthetic
shuffle law does not transfer unchanged to this finite natural-text court.

The neural core transferred `1.968741` on kin, but also `1.780461` from the
technical donor and `1.012479` to the shuffle. Raw transfer is therefore not
the fitness that a future Hebbian expert jury should maximize. The open job
is selective structural transfer under alien and shuffled controls.

## Vocabulary rent

The Dracula donor ended with 103 living units. Exact pre-crossing inspection
found 102 present in Frankenstein and only one absent: unit 58, the two-byte
form `--`. All 102 shared units remained living after 32000 Frankenstein
bytes. Unit 58 died at episode 12 with a rent age of 24157 bytes, so the
death prediction passed. It emitted no `u` receipt during the sealed 16000
byte return and the resurrection prediction failed.

The deeper result is the classification itself: this first vocabulary is
almost entirely short shared orthography, not content-specific lexical
identity. The rent mechanism worked; the preregistered return simply did not
encounter enough support to recall its sole specific form.

## Island court

Neither eight-episode 800-byte arm emitted an `r` or `q` receipt. Kin
non-refusal passed; early technical refusal failed. The travelling global
hand was byte-bi, and it remained predictive on both English targets. The
court judges measured local prediction, not a human genre label; forcing a
technical-English refusal after seeing this record would make the court less
honest, not more discerning.

## Verdict and next neural law

The four passes are kin depth, shared-vocabulary survival,
Dracula-specific death, and kin non-refusal. The four failures are alien
negativity, shuffled contextual collapse, specific resurrection within the
sealed return, and alien refusal.

No failed result licenses post-hoc corpus replacement. For the future Hebb
repair, the arena provides a sharper court: competing plasticity experts must
receive identical experience and earn *selectivity*, not merely lower loss.
A candidate that improves kin but improves a shuffled or merely stylistically
distant English arm almost as much has learned a broad byte prior, not the
interconnection-seeking instinct. Promotion still belongs to a new sealed
body with prequential fitness and an external quarantine; Hebb-v1 remains a
red arm.
