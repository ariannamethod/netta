## 5. The twin identity — a pre-seam latch is not detection

recombined, switched and moved_mid share one bit-identical price tape on
[0, 8192): the generator emits the switched life from the recombined command
prefix under the same recipient emission seed, and turn22 applies the moved
surface bijection only from byte 8192. Checked on the traces: the CUSUM
statistic and its latch flag are identical on the whole prefix for every world.

## 6. Where the tail is actually paid — the seam-to-latch burn

Cumulative gain over cold, bucketed at the latch byte. The whole slow-versus-fast
deficit is paid between the seam and the latch and saturates near 5.9 bits — the
odds stock burning off. After the latch the arms converge; what differs is whether
the remaining tail offers enough structure to repay the retained stock.
