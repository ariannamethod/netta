## What changed

The incumbent stores one pooled continuation distribution P at each of 24
selected relations. Turn33 additionally stores the same source observations
split into cases PA and PB. Both case voices begin at zero:

    e(h) = max(2^h - 1, 0)
    S = (P + e(hA) PA + e(hB) PB) / (1 + e(hA) + e(hB))
    Qearned = (1-u) P0 + u S

At hA=hB=0, S is byte-for-byte P. After each observed truth, each case wealth
is updated against P, while the inherited permission u is updated only from
the pooled shadow. Thus a case cannot rewrite the incumbent's permission
clock and receives no prior predictive mass. First visits and every
nonpositive-wealth quote remain exactly pooled.

The source archive grows from 528 to 912 bytes: 384 additional portable bytes.
Recipient state grows from 192 pooled bytes to 576 earned bytes: another 384
bytes. The preregistered price was 0.01 bit per added portable byte, or 3.84
bits per life.

Five simultaneous arms consumed the same causal P0/history tape: earned,
pooled, permuted-case residual, immediate flat3 and cold. Admission was opened
only by the pooled incumbent's 32-bit shadow and was identical for every arm.
No hazard, rho, source selector, generator or outer law changed.

## What the raw predictions say

RAW.md retains the mechanically selected help and harm rows for every partial
world. The largest help was world301 t11033: case B had earned hB=3.863 while
case A was silent at hA=-4.251; the earned paid forecast beat pooled by
4.256103 bits on that byte. The largest harm was world297 t8557: accumulated
case-B wealth hB=28.796 confidently preferred the wrong case and lost
3.690046 bits against pooled. Both source counts and raw neighborhoods are
preserved.

Across all five regimes, 443,569 selected-relation visits were observed:
177,261 silent forecasts and 266,308 active forecasts. Case voice crossed
from silent to active 3,649 times and returned to silence 3,014 times. This is
not a permanent label attached to a source case; it is revisable local
evidence.

There is one numerical boundary worth carrying forward. In worlds296 and301,
the first formal divergence from pooled is only 4.44e-16 bit after a
mathematically neutral update landed at hA=hB=+5.42e-16 in double precision.
It has no material size, first visits remain exact, and all gates stand, but a
future production organ should clamp mathematically neutral wealth to exact
zero if literal silence matters.

## Exact scope

This is evidence for local, exact-address case residuals inside the inherited
synthetic grammar/emitter family. It is not yet:

- functional similarity between differently named relations;
- natural-language or semantic transfer;
- a many-city lifetime with one continuously accumulating archive;
- a compressed case bank;
- live integration with the mouth, Body 0 or mycelium.

What it settles is one foundation question. Pooled memory need not choose
between forgetting distinctions and granting them authority in advance.
It can remain the literal baseline while distinctions earn temporary,
reversible influence from recipient evidence.
