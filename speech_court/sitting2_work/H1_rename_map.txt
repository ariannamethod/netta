H1 terminology neutralization map (sitting2 working set)

Scope: netta_mouth.c, netta.py, netta_mouth_check.c (root-level sources of
the two binaries built in A1/A2 and the py hand of B1).  g1_confirm.c and
g1_property.py exist only under speech_court/sitting2_work/ (frozen) and are
not edited.  Other root sources (netta.c, netta_check.c, transfer*.c,
mapdiag.c) are not part of the sitting2 working set and are not touched.

Decision rule: emitted artifact bytes must not change.  Identifiers,
comments, stderr and usage/help text are renamed.  Report files, trace TSV
files, speech files, stdout, and input-format strings (strcmp'd headers)
are not.  Every replacement is in-line: line counts of all three files are
unchanged, because frozen files cite netta_mouth.c:902 and :722-728
(SITTING2_PREREG.md:12,29; g1_property.py:57).
