## 2. Projection and one winner

The mouth does not recreate the source-to-destination map that made the
relations.  Its eligibility test is therefore named exactly: the
destination-byte projection of a recorded context of length one.  It is
not the full `RelationKey` and gives no authority outside that byte.

Among eligible live citizens, exactly one wins: greatest sealed
`ledger_bits`, then the complete `RelationKey` in Court 4's canonical order.
The winner may multiply the sampling weight of a candidate whose first
destination byte matches its target by exactly `1 + L`.  Citizens are never
multiplied or pooled.  This is a sampling adapter, not a claim that the
mouth reproduces Court 4's probability mixture.

The shuffled null rotates target identity bundles while preserving each
citizen's context and evidence.  If live and shuffled are byte-identical,
that is a null result, never evidence of transferred influence.  Citizen
influence is reported but is not required for NETTA to speak without the
mycelium.

## 3. The second hand

Every stream carries a token trace.  An independent reader sharing no
source with the mouth rebuilds the unit inventory, reconstructs the spoken
bytes, and scans the lived stream afresh for every choice.  Each emitted
token after the three-token lived opening must belong to the highest
non-empty support in the order quad → tri → bi → lived unigram.  The trace
is evidence about the stream, not authority to excuse an illegal token.
Restart identity covers both speech and trace.

For the sealed ear clause, the reader prices each token with its empirical
probability inside that independently reconstructed highest support.
Honest ignorance is uniform over all lived unit types.  Both totals are
divided by exact spoken bytes.

For the sealed anti-copy clause, `body0/verdict.md` supplies the already
published construction: coverage is the fraction of speech bytes in the
union of every verbatim tape match at least 32 bytes long.  The frozen void
line remains coverage `>= 0.50`; no threshold changed.
