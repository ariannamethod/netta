## What crossed, and what did not

This result repairs the specific composition question left by turn6: the
already fixed faster withdrawal rate no longer costs roughly ten percent of
unchanged benefit when the remembered candidate is the stronger joint-prefix
archive. On this batch it costs less than one percent and improves every
changed tail. That is evidence that **memory construction and the right to
continue trusting memory are separable mechanisms which can reinforce one
another**.

The representation is still exact role strings from four source lives. It
does not yet choose by functional similarity, discover a source on its own,
accumulate fifty lives, or show language-level transfer. It does not justify
altering live Netta, her mouth or mycelium. The next hand belongs to Don under
the Sol -> Don -> Astra -> Sol rotation. A useful next question is whether the
same withdrawal advantage survives when stored experience is chosen by
partial functional similarity rather than exact role identity.
