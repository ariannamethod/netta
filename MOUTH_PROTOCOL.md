# NETTA BODY 1 — THE MOUTH (contract before code)

domain: arianna-method.netta.body1-mouth-contract/v1

Order on record (operator, 2026-09-13): the organism speaks WITHOUT the
mycelium; generation is an organ of NETTA herself. The mouth iterates as
many times as it needs and earns coherence properly — long lives, not a
one-shot demo. This contract freezes the shape before any code exists.

## 1. Ground

Body 0 (`netta.c`) already speaks and stays frozen as the sealed exhibit:
its five-seed speech replays byte-identically against `body0/A/MANIFEST.tsv`
(verified 2026-09-13, 17/17). Court 4 proved that earned relations
transfer to an unseen world, and produced five living citizens with earned
bit balances. Neither artifact is touched. Body 1 is a new source file
beside them, built from their laws.

## 2. What speaks

Life on a world → earned units (Body 0's merge law: units are learned from
the lived stream, never gifted) → speech sampled ONLY over lived
continuations (supported backoff; the smoothing hand exists solely as a
red control, never as the voice). The mouth prints through its own stream
and writes nothing into the life it speaks from.

## 3. Iteration is the law, not a concession

The mouth's life length, number of passes over the world, and unit budget
are open dials. Coherence is pursued by iterating — longer lives, richer
unit inventories, deeper context — with every iteration measured by the
same sealed ruler and its speech shown. The builder loop runs without
pause between iterations (reproduce → one hypothesis → minimal change →
re-run); it stops on the court's PASS or reports the exact wall it hit.
No iteration count is promised in advance; the stop condition is the
ruler, not fatigue.

## 4. The transferred experience enters the voice

Live relation-citizens of Court 4 (state=1, earned L) advise sampling
inside their exact context and nowhere else; their weight is the L they
earned, never more. Beside the advised mouth run two controls: the same
mouth without citizens, and a shuffled-citizens null. If the citizens'
advice does not move the voice on an unseen world, that is a result and
is printed as one.

## 5. The speech court — preregistered before the first judged byte

The ruler is sealed before candidates exist: honest-ignorance bits/byte
from an independent ear over the target shore; an anti-copy census
(longest-match against the lived tape, quoted-run threshold); and the
advised/plain/null triple of §4. The word "coherence" is earned through
this court and may not be used for Body 1 speech anywhere before its
verdict. Thresholds enter only from committed records, named.

## 6. Speech is shown, always

Every gate, every report, every log entry that touches speech carries the
spoken bytes verbatim beside the numbers. A speech report made of numbers
alone is unlawful under this contract. The operator sees the voice at
every step, not a proxy for it.

## 7. Hands and probes

Two hands on the body: the builder's mouth and an independent reader that
re-derives unit inventory, replays the sampler's support law, and recounts
the census; red runs of both polarities for every gate; battery in
numbered rooms. Restart identity: a resumed life speaks the same bytes
from the same seed.

## 8. Boundaries

`netta.c`, `court4/`, `body0/`, the sealed Court-4 records, and the
mycelium branches stay untouched. The mycelium arc waits; nothing here
grants it authority or takes any. No push, no merge without the word.
