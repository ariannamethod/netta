# NETTA BODY 1 — MOUTH PROTOCOL, AMENDMENT 2: THE CORRIDOR LAW

domain: arianna-method.netta.body1-mouth-contract-amendment/v2

Written before the code it binds, after the first honest SPEECH FAIL
(all fifteen streams at anti-copy coverage 0.965661–1.000000 against the
frozen 0.50 line, ear trivially cheap at 0.024–0.039 bits/byte).  The
frozen ruler does not move.  What changes is the mouth's own speaking
law.

## The diagnosis in one sentence

A step whose lived support holds exactly one continuation type is not a
choice: it is a step along the tape's corridor, and a chain of such
steps is a quotation in progress — the 957-byte longest match of seed 7
is one corridor walked end to end.  The near-zero ear price is the same
fact seen from the other side: walking corridors costs nothing because
nothing is being said.

## The law

Let K be the corridor dial (`--corridor`, default 3; 0 disables the law
and reproduces Amendment-1 behaviour).  The mouth keeps one counter c,
starting at 0 after the three-token lived opening.

At every step, with H = the highest non-empty lived support in the
order quad → tri → bi → lived unigram:

1. If H holds two or more continuation types, the mouth chooses from H
   and resets c to 0.
2. If H holds exactly one type and c < K, the mouth takes that single
   continuation and increments c.
3. If H holds exactly one type and c >= K, the mouth descends to the
   highest support holding two or more types, chooses there, and resets
   c to 0.  If no support level holds two or more types, the mouth
   takes H's single continuation and increments c.

The descent target is still a lived support: every emitted token
remains a lived continuation of its own context at the level actually
used.  Nothing outside the lived stream can ever be spoken.

## Both hands carry the law

The token trace gains one column, `corridor`, carrying c as it stood
before the choice.  The independent reader replays the counter from its
own support scans and refuses the stream when the mouth's level or
counter disagrees with the law.  The reader prices each token inside
the support the law selected, so escaping a corridor is paid for at its
real price: the ear must rise from the parrot's near-zero toward the
honest cost of choosing.

## What would count as failure

If corridor escape does not pull coverage under the frozen 0.50 line at
any lawful dial setting, that is a measured result: the unit inventory
itself is too corridor-shaped, and the next lever is the inventory
(merges, min-pair), not the ruler.  Every measurement report shows its
streams verbatim.
